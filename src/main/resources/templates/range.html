    How the numbers are worked out

    Ammunition damage, projectile speed and air friction, clothing protection and durability, and bleeding rates come from the community DayZ Wiki (CC BY-SA), which reads them from the game's config files. Body-part multipliers are not published by Bohemia Interactive, so they are estimates. Treat results as a close guide, not an exact replay of the game.
